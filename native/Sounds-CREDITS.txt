Busy Bunny's sounds are played on real instruments, its own set.

Upright piano, bowed violin and viola sections, viola section pizzicato, cello section, Nepalese singing bells, quiet pipe organ, flute and xylophone: Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition (github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), released into the public domain under CC0 1.0.

The recordings were shortened, faded and re-encoded as Opus for Busy Bunny, which builds every sound and the background music from them.
