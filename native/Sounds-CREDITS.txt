Busy Bunny's sounds are played on real instruments.

Piano: Steinway concert grand recordings from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples (theremin.music.uiowa.edu). Free to use for any project, without restriction.

Strings (bowed violin and viola sections), violin section pizzicato, harp, glockenspiel and marimba: Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition (github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), released into the public domain under CC0 1.0.

The recordings were shortened, faded and re-encoded as Opus for Busy Bunny. The way sounds are built from them comes from Kova (github.com/ce2004/kova).
