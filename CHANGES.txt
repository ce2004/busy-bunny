0.6.2
Bug fixed: if a repeating task was scheduled on one day and due on another, like scheduled every Monday and due Friday, finishing it put next week's copy on Today straight away. The next copy is now scheduled for next Monday and due next Friday, so it stays off Today until then.
0.6.1
New feature: a Priority tab, right after Today. It lists everything urgent from every list: put on Today, top priority on its list, or due within 24 hours, most urgent first. Today stays just what you planned for today and what is due.
0.6.0
Changed: the Today tab now shows only what is for today, in two lists. To do today has what you planned for today with its do date, or put on Today yourself. Due today has what is due today or overdue. A task planned for Tuesday no longer shows up on Monday just because it is top priority or due soon.
New feature: Get ahead, at the bottom of Today, says how many more tasks are planned or due this week and opens Upcoming so you can pick one if you have the energy. When you finish everything for today, Busy Bunny tells you about them.
New feature: finishing a task before the day you planned it, or before it is due, earns bonus points, 3 for each day early.
New feature: on the Board, Today, This week and Next week each have a pick menu: things to do, things due, or both.
New feature: Upcoming shows what is planned for each day as well as what is due, and each task says the day you planned it for.
Bug fixed: if you put a task on Today with Control 1 but it had a do date later on, it stayed off Today. Now Control 1 plans it for today, and This week or Next week takes it off a do date that has come.
New feature: the morning check-in asks about each new task you type: its list, category, priority, and whether it is for today, this week or next week. They start on Today. Add several tasks works the same way, so tasks can go on different lists.
New feature: categories, like drawers inside a list. Homework can have one per class, each with its own assignments, and tags still work. Add a category is at the bottom of every list tab and in the View menu. Control N inside a category adds the task there, and Task, Put in a category moves a task into one. They used to be called sections, hidden in Settings.
New feature: subtasks are easy to find. Each task's line says how many of its steps are done, the Task field lists them by name, and Control Shift B opens them as check boxes you can check off with Space, delete, or add to.
New feature: View tab settings, at the bottom of every list tab, in the View menu, or Control Shift E. It reads the list's priority scale first, then its other settings, with buttons to change the settings, change the priority scale, or add a category.
Changed: a task no longer reads out its whole priority scale after its own priority. The scale is in View tab settings.
New feature: a sound pack for each list, for adding, saving and finishing its tasks. Bunny has hops, carrot nibbles and a warning thump; Space has blips, a launch and a beam; Coffee has a spoon clinking a cup and a pour; Stove has the burner clicking and lighting. Match the priority scale picks the one that fits, so Homework on the Coffee scale gets coffee sounds. Choose it in the list's section of Options, Settings. The new packs are in Options, Sounds too.

New feature: quiet background music while Busy Bunny is the window in front: a three-minute piece that loops, with soft strings, a harp, a little piano and a glockenspiel, at 10 percent volume. It fades out the moment you go to another program and back in when you return. Options, Sounds turns it off or changes its volume, and so does Options, Settings, General.
New feature: sounds for moving around: menus opening and closing, submenus, windows opening and closing, changing tabs, checking and unchecking check boxes, pick menus opening and closing, and Tab to the next field. They are softer than the other sounds, and each can be changed or turned off in Options, Sounds.
New feature: a sound when you add a task, and another when you save one after editing.
New feature: sounds now play over each other instead of cutting each other off.
New feature: Busy Bunny understands typing mistakes in dates and repeats, like tuesda, tomorow, wensday or evry tuesday, and many more ways of saying them: in 3 days, a week from friday, next tues, end of the month, the 20th, noon, tomorrow morning, biweekly, every other tuesday, mondays and more.
New feature: in Quick add, a date or repeat at the end of the name fills itself in. call grandma tomorrow at 3 becomes call grandma, due tomorrow at 3 pm, and Busy Bunny says what it took.
New feature: Paste tasks understands misspelled field names, statuses, task types and list names.
Changed: the update sound is now rising chords on a random instrument that fade into each other and land on a big chord, instead of single notes that cut off. Busy Bunny waits for it to finish before restarting.
Bug fixed: if Busy Bunny closed for an update while the setup guide was open, it showed an error that something went wrong. Now it just closes.

New feature: in Quick add and Edit task, Control Enter adds or saves the task from any field, even Notes. The Add and Save buttons are still there too.

Bug fixed: if a new version came out while you were in another program, Busy Bunny announced it where NVDA could not hear it, and never said it again. Now you hear it as soon as you come back to Busy Bunny, and Windows shows a notification either way.
New feature: the task form has a schedule picker for repeats: Does not repeat, Every day, Every weekday, Every week, Every 2 weeks, Every month, Every year, or your own words. For every week or every 2 weeks, check the days, like Tuesday.
New feature: for a repeating task, check Ask for a new name each time it repeats. When you finish it, Busy Bunny asks what the next one is called, and suggests the next number, so PSY chapter 9 readings becomes PSY chapter 10 readings. Press Enter to take it, or type another name.

New feature: Fun, Scoreboard (Control Shift G), Badges, Visit your bunny, Challenges and Semester Wrapped.
