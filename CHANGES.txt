0.5.0
New feature: the rest of the plan is built, everything except sync between laptops and the phone page. Help, Keyboard shortcuts lists every key.
New feature: a setup guide the first time you start: tags, class times, weekday plans, the morning check-in, how hard Busy Bunny roasts you, your bunny, and reminders. File, Setup guide brings it back.
New feature: the tabs are a list down the left side, one under another, so they never wrap into rows. Up and Down Arrow move through them.
New feature: the task form has a task type (assignment, test, meeting, event, personal goal) that shows only the fields it needs, plus a do date apart from the due date, repeat, reminder, energy, grade weight, section, notes, steps and comments.
New feature: tasks can repeat in plain words, like every Monday and Wednesday or every 2 weeks. Mark one done and the next one appears.
New feature: a heavy grade weight suggests a higher priority.
New feature: Task, Add several tasks (Control Shift N), Save as template and New from template.
New feature: Options, Settings has a Tags page: add, rename, describe and delete tags. Deleting one that is in use asks what to do with its tasks.
New feature: each list's settings have a default status, whether it shows in Today and Focus, its own finishing sound, which task types it uses, sections inside it, and a list it sits inside. Lists can be merged, archived or deleted, and Control Z undoes it.
New feature: a Board tab, one list per status.
New feature: View, Filter and sort (Control Shift F), and Clear filter.
New feature: View, Weekly review (Control Shift W) walks through next week one task at a time. View, Backlog sweep goes through tasks nobody has touched in 3 weeks.
New feature: the morning check-in (Control Shift M) asks your energy, your hours and anything new. Weekday plans suggest the day's tasks from your plan for that day.
New feature: Fun, Set the kind of day (Control Shift Y), Vacation mode and Rough day. Rough day moves the not-urgent things along and protects your streak.
New feature: unfinished Today tasks from yesterday go back to This week overnight, so nothing piles up as overdue. Settings can keep them on Today or turn this off.
New feature: reminders as Windows notifications, repeating ones too. Busy Bunny can keep running in the notification area when you close it, so reminders still come. File, Exit really quits.
New feature: Control Alt Shift A adds a task from any program, and Control Alt Shift R reads your day from any program.
New feature: Task, Push back a day (Control Shift P). The third time, it asks whether to break the task down, change it or drop it.
New feature: gentle nudges for work done but not submitted for over a day, and for anyone you have been waiting on for 3 days.
New feature: File, Paste tasks (Control Shift V) turns a list from a Claude chat into tasks, with a preview first. There is a button that copies the format for Claude.
New feature: File, Import my braille lists, Import a syllabus, Export as plain text in your braille list style, Export a backup and Restore from a backup.
New feature: after each finished task, a line of encouragement, more than 150 of them, dry or warm. Options, Settings, Fun sets the roast level from Gentle to Savage, or off, and emoji at big moments.
New feature: surprise rewards now and then: a fun fact, a roast, or something new unlocked. Finals week mode and holiday lines too.
New feature: Fun, I did a thing (Control Shift I) counts something that was never on a list. Fun, Park a thought (Control Shift K) catches a stray thought without leaving what you are doing.
New feature: points for every finished task, more for bigger and higher priority ones, with levels, a daily and weekly goal, streaks that vacation and rough days never break, and a comeback bonus.
New feature: Fun, Scoreboard (Control Shift G), Badges, Visit your bunny, Challenges and Semester Wrapped.
New feature: Fun, Start a sprint (Control Shift T), Pick one for me (Control Shift U), Quick wins (Control Shift Q), Everything is too much (Control Shift O) and Time per class. View, Low-energy mode (Control Shift L) and Shuffle mode.
New feature: big tasks with steps are bosses. Each step does damage, and beating one plays a sound.
New feature: Options, Settings, Classes for your class times, so the summary says how long until the next class. A test within 2 weeks shows as a countdown, like PSY midterm in 6 days, 3 study tasks left.
New feature: Options, Settings, Accessibility keeps a checklist of accommodations per class, with a reminder for anything still to ask.
New feature: Options, Sounds can turn any single sound off, use your own sound files, and switch sound packs: Classic, Bunny, Halloween, Winter holidays and Valentine's.
New feature: a notification from Windows when a new version of Busy Bunny is out.
New feature: while an update downloads, a random instrument climbs a scale at half volume, so you can hear it working.
Bug fixed: with NVDA set to speak typed words, typing in any Busy Bunny text box said each letter but never the word. Now it reads words like it does in Notepad.
0.4.0
New feature: Options, Settings, or Control Comma. Pick a section at the top. General has sounds, short mode, automatic update checks, how many hours you have on a typical day, and when to warn you about too many urgent tasks. Then each list has its own section: its name, its place among the tabs, and its priority scale.
New feature: add your own lists. In Settings, choose Add a new list from the Section menu, type its name and choose its settings. It gets its own tab when you press OK.
New feature: rename lists and change their order in Settings. Their tasks go with them. Control Z undoes it.
Changed: Options, Priority scales now opens Settings at the list you are on.
0.3.0
New feature: Options, Priority scales. Choose a list, then its scale: Coffee, Space, Burner, any other scale from the library, a custom one with your own levels, or None. The Levels field shows the order, like 1 Triple shot, 2 Double shot, 3 Single shot, 4 Decaf. Control Z undoes it.
New feature: tabs say each list's scale after its name, like Homework (Coffee).
New feature: the Task field also says the task's priority, like Triple shot, 1 of 4, and the order of its list's scale.
New feature: Busy Bunny checks for updates every 30 seconds, and again as soon as the internet comes back. With no internet it just waits quietly. When there is a new version it tells you once, and says how many versions behind you are, like You're 3 versions out of date.
New feature: the update window shows the changelog of every version you are missing, newest first, before you install. After updating, What's new shows just the versions you got.
Changed: Options, Check for updates when Busy Bunny starts is now Check for updates automatically.
Bug fixed: if a quiet update check was running when you chose Help, Check for updates, nothing happened. Now it always checks.
0.2.2
New feature: Edit task. Press F2 on a task, or choose Edit task from its menu (the Applications key or Shift F10) or from the Task menu. You can change its name, list, tag, status, priority, due date and size, then press Save. Control Z undoes it. Move to list and Set status are still their own items in the menu.
Changed: a task's menu now starts on its first item, Edit task, so NVDA reads it right away instead of just saying menu.
0.2.1
Bug fixed: you could not read a task by character or by word, because tasks are list items. Now press Enter on a task to go to the Task field, where the arrows read it by character and Control with the arrows by word, on braille too. Escape goes back to the list, on the same task. The Task field is also just after the lists when you press Tab.
Changed: the summary and progress lines show a cursor, so they read the same way.
0.2.0
New feature: a startup picture with the Busy Bunny logo, and a startup sound on real strings, harp and glockenspiel. Options, Sounds, Startup changes it or turns it off.
New feature: Busy Bunny has its own bunny icon in the taskbar and on the exe.
New feature: Help, View the changelog, or Shift F1, shows every change, a line each. Control Down and Control Up jump between versions. It also opens by itself after an update, so you can see what is new.
New feature: Control Page Down and Control Page Up switch tabs too, like Control Tab.
Bug fixed: if you arrowed left or right on the tab row, Busy Bunny jumped into the tab's list after the first arrow, so you could not keep arrowing through the tabs. Now the arrows stay on the tab row.
Bug fixed: if you opened Busy Bunny a second time, two copies ran and could save over each other's changes. Now the copy that is already open just comes to the front.
Bug fixed: if you typed a due time on its own in Quick add, like 3pm, Busy Bunny said it did not understand. Now it means today at 3pm.
Bug fixed: in the changelog and other lists you read, Control Down past the last version said nothing. Now it says No more headings.
0.1.0
First try of Busy Bunny: the Today tab with sample tasks, to try with NVDA and a braille display before anything else gets built.
New feature: tabs for Today, Focus, Upcoming and each of your six lists. Control Tab and Control Shift Tab move between them.
New feature: the summary line at the top, like 3 of 8 done today, test Friday, next meeting at 2, a Hop to it day. Control R reads it from anywhere in Busy Bunny.
New feature: progress for today and this week, by effort, not just by count.
New feature: the Status and Move to pick menus under the tabs change the task you chose. Nothing changes until you press Enter or leave the menu.
New feature: Quick add with Control N.
New feature: Undo with Control Z, for anything.
New feature: sounds on real piano and real strings. Options, Sounds lets you pick each one, and you hear each sound as you arrow to it.
New feature: Help, Check for updates downloads and installs a new version and keeps all your tasks.
New feature: your tasks are saved after every change, with a copy kept each day for 30 days.
