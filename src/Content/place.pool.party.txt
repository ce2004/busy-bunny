Cool water splashes everywhere as the neighbors jump in for the summer pool party. Bunny shrieks with joy and cannonballs right after them.

Warm sun, cold water, and laughter bouncing off the pool. The squirrel is already telling everyone the best kind of news.

The pony wades in at the shallow end, careful and pleased, while the ducks paddle circles around her. Bunny passes out pool noodles.

Splash. The rooster belly flops and comes up crowing about how that was on purpose. Everyone cheers anyway.

Squishy pool noodles bump into you from every side. Bunny has built a raft out of three of them and is captain now.

The hedgehog lounges on a floaty with a book held carefully above the water. Bunny swims under her and pops up to say hi.

Music drifts from the porch and the water slaps the sides as everyone dances in the pool. Bunny does a very wiggly dance.

Gerald the snail rides a tiny floating leaf across the shallow end. The whole party cheers his arrival.

Cold drips rain down as the ducks shake off on the deck. Bunny laughs and says they did that on purpose.

The owl showed up early for once and floats in the shade, sunglasses on. She says daytime parties are worth it.

A beach ball smacks the water and everyone dives for it. Bunny wins, mostly because she is the only one who did not hesitate.

Warm pool steps under your feet and cool water up to your knees. The squirrel hands you a fruit pop that is already dripping.

The pony and Bunny race across the pool. The pony wins by a nose, which is fair, since she has a long one.

Bubbles everywhere as the neighbors try to have a tea party underwater. Nobody can hear anyone, and that is the funny part.

Sunscreen, chlorine, and summer air all mix together. Bunny says this is what July smells like.

The rooster leads a game of Marco Polo, but his Polo sounds like a cock-a-doodle. Everyone finds him right away.

Splashing, giggling, and the slap of wet paws on the deck. Bunny floats on her back, totally happy.

The hedgehog finally puts down her book and joins the splash fight. Bunny says this is the best day of summer.

Cool water and warm friends. Bunny passes around a bowl of watermelon slices, sticky and sweet.

The ducks perform a synchronized swimming routine, and it is mostly chaos. Bunny scores them a perfect ten.

As the sun dips low, everyone floats quietly together for a moment. The water still feels warm from the day.

Bunny towels off, wraps up in her beach blanket, and says the party was a total success. Same time next week, the squirrel says.
