The stable gate is smooth old wood, warm from the afternoon sun. Before you even reach it, a velvet soft nose finds your hand and blows a gentle, warm puff of breath. That is Clover saying hello.

She gives a friendly nicker, low and rumbly. Her mane is long and wavy, with a few burrs tangled in from the meadow. {name} offers her an apple. It is cool and round and crisp.

Clover takes it so gently you barely feel it. Crunch. She nickers again, very pleased. {name} decides that it and Clover are now best friends. Clover agrees by resting her chin on top of {name}'s head, which squishes its ears flat. {name} does not mind at all.

title: The Long Walk

Clover takes you for a long walk this week. The lane is soft dirt under your feet, and the clip clop of her hooves keeps a slow, steady beat. The breeze smells like warm grass.

{name} rides on her back, holding onto her mane, which is silky and a bit tangly. It narrates the whole walk. "A tree. Another tree. A very confident bee."

Halfway up the hill, Clover stops and sighs happily. "This is my favorite spot," she says softly. "The grass here is the sweetest in the valley. I always think it would be a lovely place for a picnic. A really big one. With everyone."

{name} sits up straight. "A picnic," it says. "With everyone." Clover nods. Her ears swivel forward. An idea has begun.

title: The Picnic List

Back at the stable, the straw is soft and crackly, and Clover's warm side is a perfect thing to lean on. She has a pencil in her teeth, which makes her speech a bit funny.

"We need a lisht," she says. {name} takes the pencil and becomes the official writer. Clover thinks out loud.

"Apples," she says. "Lots of apples." {name} writes it down. "Carrots," says {name}, and writes that too, twice. "Blankets. Lemonade. Bread from the bakery. Songs from Fennel. A comfy spot for Gerald, who will arrive late."

The list gets longer and longer. By the end it trails off the hay bale and onto the floor. Clover reads it over and nickers happily. "It is going to be the best picnic ever," she says.

title: Finding the Spot

This week you and Clover walk all over the meadow to find the perfect picnic spot. The grass brushes your knees, soft and tickly, and crickets chirp all around.

The first spot is too bumpy. {name} lies down to test it and announces that there is a rock under its tummy. The second spot is too close to the pond, where Dot's cousins quack very enthusiastically. The third spot is perfect.

The ground is soft and flat. There is a big old tree whose leaves rustle in the breeze, making a shady spot underneath. You can hear a little stream trickling nearby.

Clover stamps one hoof gently. "Here," she says. {name} lies down again to test it. There is no rock. There is only a very happy bunny.

title: Tasting Day

The bakery is warm this week, warmer than a hug, and it smells like butter and cinnamon. The baker has set out samples on a wooden board, and the crumbs are soft and golden.

"We must choose the picnic treats," Clover says gravely. "It is a big responsibility."

{name} takes the responsibility very seriously. It tastes the apple tart, which is sticky and sweet. It tastes the carrot muffin, which is moist and warm. It tastes the honey bread, which is so soft it squishes. Then it tastes them all again, to be sure.

Clover chooses the apple tart, of course. {name} chooses everything. In the end you agree on all three. The baker laughs and wraps up a little extra for the walk home.

title: The Invitations

The post office counter is smooth and cool, and Otis is yawning his long morning yawn. "Invitations," he says sleepily. "How many?"

Clover counts with her hoof, tap tap tap. "Everyone," she says. Otis nods slowly. He gives you a stack of thick soft cards and a pencil.

You write them together. {name} adds a tiny carrot drawing on each one. On Gerald's card it writes very large letters, so Gerald has plenty of time to read them. On Pip's, it writes Please do not report this until after.

Otis tucks each card into its cubby with a soft rustle. "I will deliver them tonight," he says. "And I will come. I have been told there will be apple tart." Clover nickers happily. The picnic is real now.

title: A Very Long Table

Clank, hummm, clank. Bramble arrives at the meadow with his toolbox and a cart full of planks. The wood is smooth and smells fresh and piney.

"A picnic for everyone," he rumbles, "needs a table for everyone." He hums while he works. The hammer goes tap tap tap, and the saw goes shhhk shhhk.

{name} helps by holding nails. It drops most of them in the grass, then finds them again, one at a time, with great ceremony. Clover helps by standing very still so Bramble can rest the planks against her side.

By evening the table is done. It is very, very long, smooth on top and steady on its legs. {name} runs from one end to the other and is out of breath. "That," it says, "is a lot of table."

title: Choosing the Blankets

The stable loft is cozy and quiet this week, and full of blankets. Clover has gathered them from all over the village. You run your hands over each one.

One is woolly and thick, a bit scratchy, from Bramble. One is soft as a cloud, from Hazel. One is patchwork, with little bumpy stitches, from Pip, who made it out of old Gazette flags. One is made of the softest fleece, and smells faintly of sunshine and dog. That one is from {goldie}.

{name} lies on every blanket in turn, testing them. It rolls on each one, then sighs. "They are all perfect," it says. Clover nickers softly in agreement. "Then we will bring them all," she says, "and everyone can share."

===
title: The Night Before

The night before the picnic, Clover and {name} and you sit together on the hill. The grass is cool and soft, and the air is still warm from the day. Crickets sing all around, and far off you hear Fennel's choir practicing.

Clover's warm side rises and falls with each slow breath. "Everything is ready," she says quietly. "The table. The blankets. The tarts. The invitations."

{name} snuggles against her. "And the weather," it says. "I sniffed the air this morning. Tomorrow is picnic weather. Sunny with a strong chance of naps."

Clover laughs, a soft breathy nicker. "Thank you for helping," she says. "I have wanted this picnic for a very long time. It is better with friends."

===
title: The Big Picnic

The sun is warm on your shoulders, the blankets are soft beneath you, and the long table creaks happily under the weight of so much food. All around you is laughter, clinking cups, and the crunch of apples.

Everyone came. Hazel reads a short poem. Pip takes notes, very fast. Rocco does his new crow, and Dot quacks along. Otis dozes under the tree with tart crumbs on his feathers. Bramble hums. Fennel's choir sings. Gerald arrives during dessert, right on time for Gerald.

Clover rests her soft nose on your shoulder and blows a warm, happy puff. "The best picnic the meadow has ever heard," she whispers. {name}, very full and very content, falls asleep in the middle of a blanket, smiling.
