Dear {name},

My name is Mabel, and I am a mole. I live just under your garden, in a warm tunnel full of yarn baskets.

I am shy, so I said hello the only way I know. The apple for Clover, the bookmark for Hazel, the hammer cozy, Gerald's mitten. All me.

The leaf parcel on your doorstep is a tiny knitted bunny with floppy ears.

Would you come to tea? Knock twice on the little mound by the garden. The kettle will be on.

With love, and a little bravery,
Mabel
