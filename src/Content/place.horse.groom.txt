name: Curry comb
touch: A rubber comb with soft knobbly teeth that you rub in small circles over the warm coat, loosening dust and hair.
sound: A soft scritch, scritch, and a happy sigh from the pony.
looks: Little clouds of dust and loose hair rising from the coat.

name: Hard brush
touch: Stiff bristles in a wooden handle that you flick in short strokes the way the hair grows, sweeping the dirt away.
sound: A brisk swish, swish, swish.
looks: The coat starting to look smooth and clean.

name: Soft brush
touch: Fluffy, gentle bristles that glide along the coat in long, slow strokes, as soft as stroking a cat.
sound: A quiet whisper of bristles.
looks: The coat shining like polished wood.

name: Face cloth
touch: A damp, warm cloth you wipe gently around her eyes and soft velvet nose.
sound: A contented snuffle and a warm breath on your paw.
looks: A clean, bright face with sleepy, happy eyes.

name: Hoof pick
touch: Lift each hoof by running your paw down the leg, then scrape gently from heel to toe with the little metal hook.
sound: A scrape, scrape, and a clop as you set the hoof down.
looks: Clean hooves, free of mud and pebbles.

name: Mane and tail comb
touch: A wide toothed comb you work gently through the long hair, holding it so nothing pulls.
sound: A soft swishing tail and a happy nicker.
looks: A silky mane and a flowing tail without a single tangle.
