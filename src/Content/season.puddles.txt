Bunny finds the biggest puddle on the path and jumps. Splash. Cold water soaks right through to your toes, and they laugh so hard they snort.

Your boots squelch in the mud with every step. Bunny counts to three, and you both land in the puddle together with a big wet slap.

The rain is soft and cool on your face, and the puddle is deeper than it seemed. Water sloshes over the tops of your boots. Worth it.

Bunny tests the puddle with one careful toe. It is freezing. They jump in anyway, ears flapping.

Splash, splash, squelch. You hop from puddle to puddle down the lane, and the cold water swirls around your ankles.

The ducks watch you jump into their puddle and quack their approval. Bunny takes a bow, and water drips off their ears.

Your boots fill with water and make a little gurgle every time you take a step. Bunny thinks it is the best sound in the world.

Bunny jumps, lands, and the splash sprays all the way up to their nose. Cold drops run down their whiskers and they sneeze.

Mud squishes up between your toes, cool and soft as pudding. Bunny says this is a spa day now.

The puddle by the gate goes plop, then sploosh, then glug as Bunny stomps through it with great purpose.

Rain taps on your hood while you jump. The puddle ripples out in circles you can feel lapping against your boots.

Bunny leaps over the little puddle and lands right in the big one behind it. They meant to do that. Mostly.

You jump together and the splash soaks Gerald the snail, who seems to enjoy it very much and asks for another.

Water swishes around your boots, and the cold creeps in through the seams. Bunny wiggles their toes and squeals.

The squirrel dares Bunny to jump the widest puddle in the village. Bunny does. The splash is enormous, and so is the cheer.

Your umbrella is lying in the grass where you dropped it to jump. Rain pats your hair while the puddle sloshes cold around your ankles.

Bunny stomps, you stomp, and the puddle sends a wave of cold water down the path. The pony trots over to hear what all the noise is.

Squelch, squelch, squelch. Bunny walks in a little circle in the mud just to hear their boots do it again.

The puddle is shallow but very wide, so you shuffle through it and feel the water slide over your boots like cool silk.

One last big jump. The splash is cold and loud, and Bunny is soaked from ears to tail. Time for warm, dry socks.
