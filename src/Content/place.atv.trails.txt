name: Muddy meadow run
touch: The handlebars buzz in your paws and warm mud splatters your boots as you roll along slowly.
sound: A happy engine rumble and a big squelchy splash.
looks: Bunny grinning in a mud speckled helmet.

name: Creek splash
touch: Cool water sprays your legs as the wheels roll gently through the shallow creek.
sound: A rumble, a splash, and Bunny's delighted laugh.
looks: Sparkling water spraying in a rainbow behind you.

name: Pine forest trail
touch: The seat bounces softly over roots, and pine needles brush your arms.
sound: A steady purr of the engine and birds in the trees.
looks: Tall green pines on both sides of a winding trail.

name: Pumpkin field loop
