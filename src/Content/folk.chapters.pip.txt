title: The First Interview

Something fluffy swishes across your arm, and then a pencil starts scratching very fast. "Hello! I am Pip! I am a reporter! Well, a new reporter. Do you have any news?"

{name} thinks very hard. "I had a carrot for breakfast," it says. Pip gasps like this is the biggest story of the year. Scratch scratch scratch. "Was it crunchy? Was it orange? Did you share it?"

"Very crunchy," says {name}. "Very orange. I shared the top bit with Gerald." Pip's tail whooshes with joy. Her paws are a bit sticky with ink when she shakes your hand.

The next morning, The Acorn Gazette has a tiny headline on page four. Local Bunny Enjoys Carrot, Shares Generously. {name} asks you to read it aloud nine times.

title: Fastest Snail in the Village

Pip arrives this week out of breath, which for Pip takes some doing. "I need a sports story," she says. "The Gazette has never had sports."

{name} has an idea. It brings out Gerald the snail, who is cool and smooth and a little damp, with a shell that feels like a polished pebble. "Gerald," says {name}, "is very fast. For a snail."

Pip sets up a race track on the village green, one acorn length long. Everyone gathers. You can hear Dot the duck quacking encouragement. The race takes most of the afternoon. Gerald wins, because he is the only one in it.

The headline reads: Gerald Takes Gold. Gerald does not comment. Gerald is having a snack.

title: A Small Correction

Pip's chatter is a little quieter than usual this week. She sits on the bench beside you, and her tail is curled around her like a scarf.

"I printed that Rocco's crow can be heard from the moon," she says. "Rocco loved it. But Otis says it is not quite true."

{name} pats her paw. It is soft and only slightly inky today. "Everyone gets things a little wrong sometimes," it says. "I once told everyone that carrots grow on trees."

Pip giggles, and her chatter speeds right back up. She writes a tiny correction for page two. Rocco's crow can be heard from the hill. Rocco loves that one too. He reads it aloud to anyone who stands still long enough.

===
title: The Paper Folding Helper

The Gazette office is a hollow in an old oak tree. Inside it is warm and smells of ink and acorns, and every surface rustles with paper.

"I need a helper," says Pip. "Editors need helpers. I am an editor now. I decided this morning."

{name} is appointed Chief Folder. You fold together, side by side. The paper is smooth and crisp, and each fold goes swish, crease. {name} folds one into a small hat by accident, then puts it on. Then it folds another hat for you.

By sunset there is a tall stack of folded papers and two very fancy hats. Pip declares it the most productive day in Gazette history. Her pencil is still tucked behind her ear, where it always lives.

===
title: The Printing Press

Clank, hummm, clank. Bramble rolls something heavy into the oak hollow this week. You reach out and feel cool metal, smooth wooden handles, and a big round roller that turns with a soft creak.

"A printing press," Bramble rumbles. "Found it in the barn behind the bakery. Needed a bit of oil."

Pip makes a sound that is not quite a word. It is mostly a squeak. She turns the handle and the press goes ka chunk, ka chunk, and out slides a page, warm and smelling of fresh ink.

"No more handwriting every copy," she whispers, hugging the roller. {name} hugs it too, to be supportive. It now has an ink stripe across its tummy. Pip prints a photo of that stripe. It is the first picture in Gazette history.

===
title: The Weather Column

"The Gazette needs a weather column," Pip announces, her tail twitching. "And I have chosen my expert."

She turns to {name}. {name} sits up very straight. "I accept," it says. "I have been studying weather my whole life. Mostly from my bed."

Each morning {name} sticks its nose out of the burrow and sniffs. It feels the air on its whiskers. Cool and damp means puddle hopping weather. Warm and still means nap weather. Breezy means ear flapping weather.

Pip prints every word. Readers love it. Rocco says the nap weather forecasts are the most accurate in the valley. Hazel cuts them out and keeps them in a scrapbook. {name} starts wearing a tiny scarf, because weather experts should look the part.

===
title: Ask Pip

This week the Gazette has a new section called Ask Pip. Pip sits at the tea house with a big basket, and neighbors drop in folded questions. The tea house is warm, the cups clink softly, and the basket rustles with paper.

Dear Pip, says one, how do I get my door to stop squeaking? Signed, Bunny. Pip writes back: Dear Bunny, ask Bramble, and also, I love that squeak.

Dear Pip, says another, how do I tell my rooster friend his new crow sounds like a kettle? Signed, a Friend. Pip writes back: Dear Friend, tell him it is a very musical kettle.

{name} reads the whole column twice. "You are very good at this," it says. Pip's tail whooshes. "I have had practice," she says. "Everyone tells me everything."

===
title: The Special Edition

Ka chunk, ka chunk, ka chunk. The press has been going since sunrise. Pip's paws are covered in ink and her ears are standing straight up with excitement.

"A special edition," she says. "Thicker than ever. A page for every neighbor." She hands you a copy. It is heavy and crisp and still warm from the press.

There is a page about Hazel's quiet reading hour. A page about Otis and the night mail. A page about Clover's walks. A whole page of Fennel's choir, with the words to a new song. And right in the middle, a page that just says Our Friends, with a little drawing of a round bunny and someone beside it. {name} holds it to its chest. "We are in the news," it whispers.

===
title: Read Aloud at the Tea House

The tea house is full this week. You can feel the warmth from the stove and hear cups clinking, spoons stirring, and lots of happy chatter.

Pip climbs onto a chair. "Today," she announces, "the Gazette will be read aloud. For everyone. By me. Quickly."

She reads every story, and she reads very fast, so Hazel gently asks her to slow down. Pip slows down to only a little fast. Everyone laughs in the right places. When she reaches the weather column, the whole room turns to look at {name}, who bows so deeply that it rolls gently off its cushion onto a softer cushion.

Pip finishes to a big round of applause. Her tail is puffed up like a feather duster. "Best day," she whispers. "Best day in acorn news."

===
title: Friends of the Year

The final Gazette of the season lands on your doorstep with a soft thump. It is the thickest one yet, and it smells of ink and oak.

{name} unrolls it with trembling excitement. The front page has one giant headline, in raised letters Pip pressed extra deep so you can feel them. It says: Friends of the Year.

Under it is a story about a small round bunny and its dear friend, who finished task after task, visited every neighbor, and made the whole village a little cozier just by being in it.

Pip pops her head in the door, chattering faster than ever. "Do you like it? I wrote it myself. I checked every fact twice. Otis checked them three times." {name} hugs her so hard her tail fluffs out. "Every word is true," it says.
