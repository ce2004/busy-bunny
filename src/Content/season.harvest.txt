The judges feel how heavy your pumpkins have grown, round and cool, and pin a big ribbon right on the biggest one.

Your apple tree is so full the branches droop, and the hedgehog gives you a ribbon for the sweetest crop of the fall.

The owl walks your rows of squash, knocking on each with a hollow thunk, and hands you a ribbon for steady growing.

All your work this fall has turned into baskets of corn, and the husks rustle as the pony gives you a ribbon.

The squirrel tastes one of your carrots, crunchy and earthy, and gives you a ribbon for most improved garden.

The judges smell your herb patch, warm and spicy, and give you a ribbon for most fragrant harvest.

Your potatoes are lumpy and heavy in the basket, and the rooster gives you a ribbon for best root vegetables.

The ducks find your cranberries, firm and tart, and give you a ribbon for brightest harvest.

The hedgehog says she watched your garden grow all fall, one little bit at a time, and gives you a ribbon for patience.

Your sunflower heads are heavy with seeds, and Gerald the snail gives you a ribbon for tallest stalks.

The pony feels the bumpy gourds lined up on your fence and gives you a ribbon for most interesting shapes.

The judges all agree your garden grew beautifully this fall, and give you a big satin ribbon that crinkles when you hold it.
