A marble clinks into the jar.

Clink. The jar is a little fuller.

A blue marble rolls in and settles with a tick.

Plink. Another one for the jar.

The marble spins around the bottom before it lands.

Clink, clink. Bunny counts it twice to be sure.

A swirly green one this time. Bunny likes that one.

The jar rings like a tiny bell.

In it goes, cool and smooth.

A cat's-eye marble drops in and winks.

Bunny holds the jar up and gives it a happy shake.

Tick. The marbles shuffle to make room.

A red one, bright as a berry.

The jar sings a little glassy note.

Another marble, another win.

Clink. That sound never gets old.

The marbles click against each other like applause.

One more. The pile is getting tall.

A marble slides down the side with a soft whirr.

Bunny drops it in from very high, for drama. Clink.

The jar feels heavier now. In a good way.

Plunk. Right on top of the pile.
