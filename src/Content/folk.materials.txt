name: twigs
touch: Thin, dry and light, with little knobbly bumps along each one.
sound: They snap with a crisp pop and rattle together in a bundle.

name: yarn
touch: Soft and springy, with a gentle twist you can feel when it runs through your paws.
sound: A quiet fuzzy whisper as the ball unrolls.

name: buttons
touch: Small, smooth and round, each with little holes you can poke a claw into.
sound: They clatter like tiny raindrops in a tin.

name: pinecones
touch: Bumpy, woody scales that tickle your paw pads.
sound: A dry crackle when you squeeze one.

name: ribbon
touch: Silky and cool on one side, slightly grainy on the other.
sound: A soft swish as it slides through a loop.

name: acorn caps
touch: Little rough cups with a tiny stem on top, like miniature hats.
sound: A light clicky tap when you stack them.

name: driftwood
touch: Silky smooth and surprisingly light, worn soft by the sea.
sound: A hollow, friendly knock when two pieces meet.

name: felt
touch: Thick, fuzzy and soft, with no stretch at all.
sound: Almost silent, just a soft pff when you fold it.

name: beads
touch: Round and glassy, rolling around in your paw like peas.
sound: They tinkle and click as they slide onto a string.

name: moss
touch: Springy, damp and cushiony, like a tiny green pillow.
sound: A soft squish and nothing more.

name: bark
touch: Rough and ridged on the outside, smooth on the inside.
sound: A crunchy crackle when it bends.

name: feathers
touch: So light they barely feel like anything, with soft edges that tickle your nose.
sound: A whisper so faint you have to hold your breath to hear it.
