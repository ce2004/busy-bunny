name: A daisy
touch: Thin soft petals around a bumpy center, like a tiny pincushion.
sound: A faint rustle in the breeze.

name: A bluebell
touch: Little hanging bells, smooth and cool, all along one stem.
sound: Almost a tinkle when the wind brushes them.

name: A clover blossom
touch: A round fluffy head made of tiny tubes.
sound: Bees humming as they visit.

name: A buttercup
touch: Glossy petals that feel waxy and smooth.
sound: A tiny tick as a stem springs back.

name: A poppy
touch: Petals as thin and crinkly as tissue paper.
sound: A papery flutter.

name: A violet
touch: Small velvety petals and heart-shaped leaves.
sound: Quiet. Violets are shy.

name: A sunflower
touch: A big rough face full of seeds, with a fuzzy stem.
sound: A creak as it turns toward the sun.

name: A forget-me-not
touch: Tiny round petals in a little cluster, delicate as crumbs.
sound: A whisper among the grass.

name: A lavender sprig
touch: Bumpy little buds along a stiff stem.
sound: A soft swish when you brush it.
looks: It smells calm and sweet.

name: A black-eyed Susan
touch: Rough petals around a firm domed middle.
sound: Leaves rubbing together.

name: A wild rose
touch: Silky petals and, careful, a few small thorns on the stem.
sound: A faint hum of a visiting bee.

name: A cornflower
touch: Frilly, spiky petals like a little crown.
sound: A dry rustle in the wind.

name: A foxglove
touch: Tall stems with soft tube-shaped flowers you could fit a fingertip in.
sound: Bumblebees buzzing in and out.

name: A dandelion puff
touch: A ball of fluff so light it tickles.
sound: A soft breath, and the seeds float away.

name: A morning glory
touch: A smooth trumpet that is open in the morning and closed by evening.
sound: A vine creaking as it climbs.

name: A Queen Anne's lace
touch: A flat lacy umbrella of tiny flowers.
sound: A whisper in the field.

name: A goldenrod
touch: Fluffy spikes of tiny flowers, slightly sticky.
sound: Busy bees and a buzzing chorus.

name: A honeysuckle
touch: Thin trumpet flowers along a twisting vine.
sound: A hummingbird's wings, very fast.
looks: It smells like summer.

name: A primrose
touch: Five soft rounded petals and crinkly leaves.
sound: A drip of dew falling off a leaf.

name: A thistle
touch: Prickly leaves and a soft tufted purple top. Touch gently.
sound: A crackle as it sways.

name: A snowdrop
touch: A small drooping bell, cool as frost.
sound: The quiet of early spring.

name: A marigold
touch: Ruffled layers of petals, thick and soft.
sound: A crisp rustle.
