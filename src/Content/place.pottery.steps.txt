name: Centering the clay
touch: Cool wet clay spins under your palms, wobbly at first, until you press steadily and it turns smooth and still beneath your paws.
sound: The soft whir of the wheel and a wet shhh of slippery clay.
looks: A smooth gray mound spinning perfectly in the middle.

name: Opening it
touch: Your thumbs press down gently into the center, feeling the clay give way as a soft hollow forms.
sound: A quiet squish and the steady hum of the wheel.
looks: A little well in the middle of the spinning clay.

name: Pulling up the walls
touch: One paw inside, one outside, you feel the wall rise slowly between your fingers, thinning and growing taller.
sound: A wet glide and a gentle slip slap of water.
looks: A cup shape rising from the wheel like magic.

name: Trimming
touch: The clay is firm now, like cool leather, and a little metal tool shaves smooth curls from the base.
sound: A soft scraping and curls of clay plopping off.
looks: A neat foot ring and a tidy bottom on your pot.

name: Glazing
touch: You dip the pot into a cool, silky glaze that feels like thick cream coating your paws.
sound: A gentle glug as the pot goes in and drips back out.
looks: A pot covered in a dull coat that will turn glossy and bright after firing.
