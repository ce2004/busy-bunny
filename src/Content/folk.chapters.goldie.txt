title: A Soft Hello

The first thing {name} notices is warmth. Something big and fluffy settles down beside it on the village green, and the grass gives a little under the weight. Then comes a sound, a soft jingle, like tiny bells in a pocket.

It is {goldie}, the golden retriever. Her fur is thick and sun-warm, and her harness is soft leather with little rings that jingle when she moves. She sniffs {name}'s ear very politely, as if to say hello, I am glad you are here.

{name} leans against her side. She does not mind at all. They sit together and listen to the bees in the clover, and for a long, easy while, neither of them needs to do anything else.

{goldie} pads over, harness jingling softly. She sniffs the other mitten, very carefully. Then she turns and walks, slow and sure, to the coat hooks by the door. She sits and looks back at {name}.

title: Always Glad

It is the last evening of the season. The fire crackles, and the burrow is warm and smells like cocoa. {goldie} lies on the rug, and her harness jingles once, softly, as she stretches.

{name} thinks about all the walks they took this season. The foggy lane. The noisy pond. The lost mitten. The sun patch. The first snow. {goldie} was there for every one, calm and warm and steady.

{name} curls up next to her and says thank you. {goldie} does not say anything, because she is a dog. But she rests her head on {name}'s paw, and her tail thumps the rug, slow and happy. It means the same thing it always means. I am glad you are here. I will always be glad you are here.
