title: The Famous Crow

The fence post is cool and a little dewy under your hand, and the morning air smells like grass. Then, right beside your ear, comes the loudest sound in the village. Cock a doodle doooo.

{name}'s ears shoot straight up and its fur fluffs into a perfect ball. "Good morning," says Rocco, very pleased with himself. He puffs out his chest so you can feel how springy his feathers are. "That," he says, "was my crow. People travel for miles to hear it. Well. Some people. Dot came from the pond once."

{name} slowly unfluffs. "It is very loud," it says politely. Rocco bows. "Thank you," he says. "I practice."

title: A Brand New Crow

Rocco looks thoughtful this week, which makes his comb flop to one side. You can feel it, soft and floppy, when he leans down to whisper.

"I have crowed the same crow every morning for years," he says. "It is a classic. But I want a new one. A crow with two parts. A crow with a tune."

{name} gasps. "Like a song?" Rocco nods so hard his tail feathers rustle. "Exactly like a song. Every morning, the whole village will wake up humming."

He takes a deep breath and tries it. What comes out sounds like a squeaky gate having a lovely time. {name} claps anyway. Rocco is undaunted. "Practice," he says. "That is all it needs. Lots of practice."

===
title: Practice in the Barn

Rocco practices in the barn this week, where the hay is soft and prickly and smells like summer. The walls make every sound echo.

Cock a doodle dee? Cock a doodle doo dee? Cock a doodle hoo? That last one wakes Otis, who is napping in the rafters. "I am on the night shift," Otis says, from somewhere above. "Please crow in a lower key."

{name} hops up on a hay bale and becomes the official listener. After each try it gives a score. "That one was a seven," it says. "That one had a lovely wobble." "That one sounded like a duck. In a good way."

By sunset Rocco has a crow that is almost a tune. He looks very proud, and a little hay is stuck in his feathers.

===
title: A Lesson from Fennel

Down by the pond the air is cool and soft, and the reeds whisper against each other. Fennel the frog sits on his lily pad, smooth and green, tapping a tiny pitch pipe.

"Singing," says Fennel in his deep round voice, "comes from the tummy." He pats Rocco's tummy. Rocco looks surprised. "Breathe in. Feel it fill up like a balloon. Then let it out slowly."

Rocco breathes in so much he wobbles. {name} breathes in too, to help, and gets the hiccups. Fennel does not mind. He hums a note. Rocco copies it. Then a second note, higher. Rocco copies that too.

"Two parts," Fennel says, smiling. "You have them. Now you just need to put them together." Rocco's comb stands straight up with joy.

title: Sunday Secret

On Sunday morning the village is very quiet. No crow at all. Just the soft rustle of leaves and Gerald slowly munching a lettuce leaf.

{name} tiptoes to the henhouse. Inside it is warm and smells of straw. And there, tucked under a fluffy blanket with a tiny sleep mask, is Rocco. Snoring. A gentle, whistly snore.

He opens one eye. "Ah," he says. "You have discovered my secret. On Sundays, I sleep in." He sighs. "A rooster must rest his voice."

{name} nods very seriously. "I will tell no one," it says. "Not even Pip." Rocco looks so relieved that his feathers relax all at once, like a pillow letting out air. Then {name} crawls under the blanket too. It is Sunday, after all.

===
title: Ducks Join In

Rocco practices by the pond this week. The grass is soft and damp under your knees, and the water laps gently at the bank.

Cock a doodle doo dee. It is getting better. Then, from the water, comes a cheerful quack. Then another. Dot the duck has paddled over, along with her many cousins, and they quack along on every second note.

Rocco is startled at first. Then he tries again. Cock a doodle, quack, doo dee, quack. It is surprisingly catchy. {name} starts bobbing its head. Fennel, on his lily pad, adds a deep ribbit at the end.

"A backing chorus," says Rocco, amazed. "I did not know I needed one." Dot quacks proudly. It sounds like she is saying, you are welcome.

===
title: Almost There

Rocco sits on the fence post this week, and the wood is warm from the sun. He sings his new crow over and over, softly, just for practice.

It goes up, then curls around, then comes down like a ribbon. It is very nearly a tune now. {name} sways along. Even Clover, walking past with her slow clip clop, stops to listen and gives an approving little nicker.

"One more piece," Rocco says. "The ending. It needs a flourish." He tries a few. One ends with a squeak. One ends with a hiccup. One ends with him sneezing on a feather.

{name} has a suggestion. "What if it ends," it says, "with a happy little cluck?" Rocco tries it. It is perfect. He sounds delighted with himself, and so is everyone else.

===
title: A Fluttery Feeling

This week Rocco's feathers feel a little ruffled, like they cannot decide which way to lie. "The big morning is soon," he says. "What if the whole village wakes up and does not like it?"

{name} sits beside him on the fence. Its fur is warm against his wing. "Everyone already likes you," it says. "Your old crow, your new crow, even your sneezing crow."

Rocco thinks about this. Bramble, passing with his clanking toolbox, stops to say he hums Rocco's practice crow while weeding. Hazel says she hears it through the library window and taps her pencil along. Pip says she has already written the headline.

Rocco's feathers settle down smooth again. "Well," he says, puffing up, "in that case. I suppose I am ready."

===
title: The Dress Rehearsal

The night before the big morning, Rocco holds a dress rehearsal on the village green. The grass is cool, and the evening air smells like clover and bread from the bakery.

He wears a small scarf for the occasion. It is very soft and slightly too long, so it trails on the ground behind him. Dot and her cousins line up for their quacks. Fennel sits nearby with his pitch pipe.

{name} counts them in. "One, two, three, crow!" Cock a doodle, quack, doo dee, quack, ribbit, cluck. It rings out across the green like a little song.

There is a quiet moment. Then everyone who came to watch claps and cheers. Rocco bows so low his scarf goes over his head. He is beaming underneath it.

===
title: The New Morning

The very next morning you are cozy in bed, blanket warm and soft, when it begins. Cock a doodle, quack, doo dee, quack, ribbit, cluck.

It floats over every rooftop in the village. Windows creak open. Somewhere, Hazel laughs her teaspoon laugh. Pip's chatter starts up at once. Otis, just home from the night shift, hoots along sleepily before tucking in.

{name} bursts out of its burrow and does the official bunny dance for big news, three circles and a hop. All across the village, neighbors are humming the new crow over their breakfast.

Rocco stands on his fence post, chest puffed, feathers shining. "Good morning, everyone," he calls. Then, a little quieter, just for you and {name}: "Thank you for listening to all the squeaky ones."
