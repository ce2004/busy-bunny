Your line tugs, and you reel in a little silver fish, cool and wriggly. You thank it and let it go with a splash.

You pull up an old rubber boot full of pond water, cold and squishy. The ducks say it counts as a catch.

A fat golden carp splashes up onto the dock, slippery and strong, then swims off happily when you set it free.

You catch a tiny minnow that tickles your paw as you hold it, and you let it slip back into the water.

Something heavy tugs your line, and up comes a glass bottle with a note inside that says hello from the other side of the pond.

You reel in a frog who looks very surprised and hops off with a ribbit. The judges give you a ribbon for friendship.

A big whiskery catfish comes up on your line, smooth and heavy, and then glides back into the deep.

You pull up a clump of soft green pond weed, and the squirrel declares it the best salad of the day.

A trout splashes cold water all over your paws, then darts back into the pond the second you let go.

You catch a teacup, still in one piece, and the hedgehog says it must have floated away from a picnic.

Your line wiggles and you reel in a little sunfish, warm from the shallows, and you let it go to bask some more.

A turtle grabs your bait and holds on, then lets go with a very polite blink and swims off.

You catch a bottle with a message that says you are doing great, keep going. Nobody knows who wrote it.

You reel in a long eel, slippery and wriggly, and everyone squeals as you let it slide back in.

Your bobber dips and you pull up a shiny pebble tangled in the line, smooth and cool.

A big bass leaps out of the water and lands back in before you can even touch it. The crowd cheers anyway.

You catch a lost toy duck, squeaky and soft, and the real ducks are very happy to have it back.

A small fish tickles your paw with its fins as you hold it, then flicks its tail and swims away.

At the end of the day the owl gives everyone a ribbon, and yours is for the friendliest catch.

You pull up a sunken hat, dripping wet, and Gerald the snail says it is exactly the hat he lost last summer.
