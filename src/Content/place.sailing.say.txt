Cool spray mists your whiskers as the little boat glides out onto the lake. The sail snaps full of wind and the water laps against the hull. Semester break has officially begun.

The wooden rail is smooth and warm under your paws. Bunny steers while the sail flutters and the ducks paddle alongside, quacking hello.

A gentle breeze tugs at your ears as the boat rocks softly. Water splashes against the side, and Bunny says the lake is a perfect break from classes.

Sun on your fur, wind in your face, and the creak of the mast. Bunny holds the rope and shouts, "Full speed ahead, very slowly."

The boat sways under you like a cradle. Little waves slap the hull, and far across the lake you can hear the frogs cheering you on.

You trail a paw in the cool water as the boat drifts along. The sail ripples, the ropes creak, and nobody has any homework today.

A soft breeze fills the sail with a whump. The boat leans just a little, and Bunny giggles as the water sparkles all around.

Smooth planks, cool spray, and the slow slap of waves. You sail across the lake and back, and the whole break feels wide open.

The rope is rough and salty in your paws. Bunny tugs it, the sail swells, and the little boat skims across the lake like a skipping stone.

Wind whispers in the sail, and the water gurgles under the bow. Bunny packed carrot sandwiches, of course, and they taste better on a boat.

Warm sun, cool breeze, gentle rocking. The lake carries you both to the far shore, where the pony waves from the dock.

Water drips from the paddle back into the lake with a soft plink. The breeze picks up, the sail fills, and off you go on your break adventure.

The deck feels warm and steady. Bunny ties a perfect knot, then a less perfect one, and the boat sails merrily on.

A splash of cool lake water lands on your nose. Bunny apologizes, then splashes again on purpose. The sail flaps happily overhead.

You feel the boat lift over a small wave and settle back down. The water chuckles against the hull, and the sky goes on forever.

The breeze smells of pine and lake water. Your boat glides past the reeds, where the ducks call out, "Happy break."

Soft cushions in the bottom of the boat, a lazy breeze in the sail. Bunny lies back, ears flopped, and declares this the best break ever.

The tiller hums under your paw as the boat turns. Little waves sparkle and slap, and the far shore gets closer and closer.

Cool, fresh air rushes past as the sail catches a gust. The boat zips across the water, and Bunny whoops with joy.

The gentle rocking makes you sleepy. Water laps, ropes creak, and the sun warms the deck. Semester break is off to a lovely start.

A dragonfly buzzes past your ear. The boat drifts across the calm lake, and Bunny trails a carrot in the water, just to see.

You step back onto the dock, legs a little wobbly and heart very happy. The lake glitters behind you, and Bunny is already planning the next sail.
