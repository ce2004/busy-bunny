name: Peppermint
touch: Soft fuzzy leaves with crinkly edges that leave your paw smelling fresh.
sound: A crisp snap when you pick a sprig.
looks: Bright green leaves on square stems.
tea: Cool and bright, it wakes up your nose and settles your tummy.

name: Chamomile
touch: Tiny daisy flowers with soft round centers and feathery leaves.
sound: A whispery rustle in the breeze.
looks: Little white and yellow blossoms in a fluffy patch.
tea: Mild and honey sweet, it feels like being tucked into bed.

name: Lavender
touch: Slender stems with little bumpy flower spikes and soft silvery leaves.
sound: Bees humming lazily all around.
looks: Purple wands swaying in a row.
tea: Floral and calm, like breathing out after a long day.

name: Lemon balm
touch: Soft wrinkly heart shaped leaves that smell of lemon when rubbed.
sound: A gentle rustle as the leaves brush together.
looks: Bright leafy mounds of light green.
tea: Light and lemony, it lifts your mood like a sunny morning.

name: Rosemary
touch: Thin, needle like leaves, firm and a little piney.
sound: A woody snap.
looks: A small shrub with dark green needles and tiny blue flowers.
tea: Warm and woodsy, it makes your thoughts feel clear.

name: Sage
touch: Velvety soft gray green leaves, like fuzzy little tongues.
sound: A soft brush of leaves.
looks: Silvery green leaves in a cozy clump.
tea: Earthy and soothing, like a warm scarf for your throat.

name: Thyme
touch: Tiny round leaves on wiry little stems that tickle your paw.
sound: A faint crackle.
looks: A low carpet of small green leaves and pink flowers.
tea: Gentle and savory, warming from the inside out.

name: Basil
touch: Big smooth glossy leaves that feel cool and a little puffy.
sound: A soft pop when you pinch off a leaf.
looks: Rich green leaves stacked like little umbrellas.
tea: Sweet and peppery, a surprising cozy treat.

name: Lemongrass
touch: Long stiff grassy blades with a firm bulb at the bottom.
sound: A dry swish in the wind.
looks: Tall pale green grass in a big tuft.
tea: Zesty and bright, like a little sunshine in a cup.

name: Ginger root
touch: Knobbly firm root with a thin papery skin.
sound: A crisp snap when you break off a piece.
looks: A tan root shaped like a funny little paw.
tea: Spicy and warm, it makes your toes feel toasty.

name: Rose hips
touch: Smooth firm little berries, round and shiny.
sound: A light rattle in the basket.
looks: Glossy red orange berries left after the wild roses bloom.
tea: Tart and fruity, like a cozy fruit punch.

name: Spearmint
touch: Smooth pointed leaves, softer and gentler than peppermint.
sound: A tiny crinkle when you rub a leaf.
looks: Light green leaves on tall stems.
tea: Sweet and mellow mint, refreshing without being too strong.
