name: carrot cake
smell: Warm cinnamon, brown sugar and sweet carrot.
touch: A soft, springy crumb under a thick layer of smooth cream cheese frosting.
sound: The fork sinks in with a gentle squish.
steps: Stir grated carrots, flour, sugar, eggs and cinnamon together, then bake until golden. Frost it once it has cooled.

name: honey oat bars
smell: Toasty oats and warm honey.
touch: Chewy and a little sticky, with crunchy edges.
sound: A soft crunch at the corners and a quiet chew in the middle.
steps: Mix oats, honey and melted butter, press it into a pan and bake until the edges turn golden. Cut into squares.
