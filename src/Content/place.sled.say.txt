Cold air rushes past your ears as the sled whooshes down the hill. Bunny squeals with delight and holds on tight.

The wooden sled is smooth and chilly under your paws. One push, and you are flying down the snowy slope with Bunny.

Snow sprays up in a sparkly cloud as the sled zooms along. Bunny laughs so hard she snorts.

Crunch, crunch, crunch, up the hill you go, dragging the sled behind. Then whoosh, all the way back down.

Icy wind tickles your whiskers as you pick up speed. Bunny shouts faster, faster, and the hill agrees.

The sled bumps over a little mound and you both bounce. Bunny lands giggling in a heap of powder.

Soft snow cushions the end of the run as the sled slides to a gentle stop. Bunny says again, again.

Mittens on, ears tucked, and down you go. The runners hiss across the snow like a whisper.

Bunny sits in front and you hold on behind her, warm and snug. The hill rushes up to meet you, smooth and fast.

The ducks slide down the hill on their tummies right behind you. Bunny calls it a parade on ice.

Fresh snow squeaks under the runners. Bunny leans left and the sled curves around a snowy bush.

Cold flakes sting your cheeks in the nicest way. Bunny's ears flap behind her like little scarves.

The pony gives you a gentle push from the top, and away you go. Bunny waves to her the whole way down.

Whoosh, bump, whoosh. The sled glides over every little dip, and your tummy does a happy flip.

Snow crunches as you trudge back up, cheeks rosy and paws chilly. Bunny says the walk up makes the ride down worth it.

The sled spins in a slow circle at the bottom, and you both tumble out into the soft snow. Bunny makes a snow angel right away.

Frosty air fills your nose as the sled speeds up. Bunny holds her paws up high like she is on a roller coaster.

The runners squeak and hiss on packed snow. Bunny steers with her feet and mostly goes straight, which counts.

Gerald the snail watches from the bottom with a tiny scarf on. He cheers every single run.

The hill is quiet except for your laughter and the swish of the sled. Bunny says winter was made for this.

Snow clings to your fur, cold and fluffy. Bunny shakes it off and grabs the rope for one more trip up.

One last run, the fastest yet. The sled glides to a stop, and Bunny flops back in the snow, happy and breathless.
