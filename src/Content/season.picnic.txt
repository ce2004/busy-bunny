Bunny spreads the checked blanket on the grass. It is soft and warm from the sun, and the bread in the basket is still warm too.

The basket creaks as Bunny opens it. Inside are warm bread, cool apples, and a jar of jam that is a little sticky at the lid.

You tear off a piece of crusty bread and it crackles. A line of ants marches off with a crumb bigger than all of them put together.

The checked cotton blanket is a bit rough and warm under your hands. Bees hum in the clover nearby, minding their own business.

Bunny pours lemonade into cups, cold and fizzy, and the ice clinks. The breeze tugs at one corner of the blanket.

The ants have formed a tiny parade, carrying off a cracker crumb in a long, careful line. Bunny hums them a marching song.

A soft breeze carries the smell of warm bread and cut grass. Bunny passes you a slice, still soft and springy in the middle.

The cheese is cool and crumbly, the bread is warm, and the sun is warm on your shoulders. A very good lunch.

Bunny bites into a crunchy carrot, and the crunch carries right across the meadow. The ducks look up from the pond.

The squirrel shows up uninvited, sits on the edge of the blanket, and asks if anyone is going to finish their nuts.

Grass pokes up through the blanket in little tufts. Bunny smooths it down with her paw, and it pops right back up.

You hold a warm muffin, the top crumbly and sweet. An ant climbs onto your knee to see what is going on up there.

The sun warms the blanket until it feels like a toasty bed. Bunny lies back and declares it nap time, right after cake.

A strawberry, cool and sweet, bursts when you bite it. Juice runs down your chin, and Bunny hands you a napkin.

The breeze flips the napkins over one by one. Bunny chases them across the meadow, ears flapping and paws thumping.

Three ants carry away a crumb of shortbread, very slowly and very proudly. Bunny gives them a little round of applause.

The pony wanders over, sniffs the bread, and gets a crust. She chews happily, her warm breath puffing on your hand.

Bunny pours tea from a flask, steaming and sweet. The cup is warm and heavy in your hands, and the blanket is soft.

Sandwiches wrapped in waxed paper crinkle as you open them. Bunny's has carrot in it, of course, and she is very happy about that.

When the basket is empty, Bunny shakes out the checked blanket. Crumbs fly, and a whole family of ants cheers quietly.
