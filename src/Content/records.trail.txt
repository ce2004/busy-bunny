name: The trailhead
touch: Packed dirt under your paws and a wooden sign with carved letters.
sound: Birds chattering, everyone excited to start.

name: The little bridge
touch: Wooden planks that give a little with each step.
sound: A brook trickling underneath.

name: The pine grove
touch: Soft needles underfoot, springy and quiet.
sound: Wind hushing through tall branches.

name: The mossy log
touch: A fallen log covered in cushiony moss. A good place to sit.
sound: A woodpecker tapping somewhere far away.

name: The blackberry bramble
touch: Prickly canes with a few sweet berries if you reach carefully.
sound: Buzzing bees.

name: The stepping stones
touch: Smooth flat rocks across a shallow creek, cool and wet.
sound: Splashes as you hop from one to the next.

name: The meadow
touch: Tall grass brushing your shoulders, warm from the sun.
sound: Crickets singing.

name: The old oak
touch: Deep ridged bark you can feel like a map.
sound: Leaves rustling high above.

name: The halfway bench
touch: A wooden bench worn smooth by many travelers.
sound: Your own breathing, slowing down. You are about halfway.

name: The windy ridge
touch: Cool air on your face and gravel crunching underfoot.
sound: The wind whistling past your ears.

name: The berry stand
touch: A little wooden counter with a basket of berries.
sound: A neighbor humming while sorting fruit.

name: The fern hollow
touch: Feathery ferns brushing your legs and damp, cool air.
sound: Water dripping off leaves.

name: The rope bridge
touch: Rough rope railings that sway gently.
sound: The creak of the rope and a river far below.

name: The cave overhang
touch: Cool rock above you and dry sand below.
sound: Your voice echoing back softly.

name: The sunny clearing
touch: Warm flat rocks perfect for a rest.
sound: A breeze and a distant woodpecker.

name: The waterfall view
touch: Fine mist on your face.
sound: A steady roar of falling water.

name: The switchbacks
touch: The path zigzags uphill, with a handrail of smooth wood.
sound: Pebbles skittering away under your steps.

name: The lookout
touch: A stone wall at waist height, cool and solid.
sound: Quiet, open air. You can feel how far you have come.

name: The pine cone hill
touch: Pine cones everywhere, rolling under your paws.
sound: Crunch, crunch, crunch.

name: The last stream
touch: A shallow crossing with warm sunlit stones.
sound: Water chuckling over pebbles.

name: The lantern path
touch: Smooth flat stones lit by little lanterns that feel warm as you pass.
sound: Soft music from the finish ahead.

name: The finish line
touch: A ribbon across the path that you break with your chest.
sound: Cheering neighbors. You made it to the end of the semester.
