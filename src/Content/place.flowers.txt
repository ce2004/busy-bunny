name: Daisy
touch: A ring of thin smooth petals around a soft bumpy button center.
sound: A tiny rustle as the stem bends.
looks: White petals and a sunny yellow middle.

name: Buttercup
touch: Five waxy petals, smooth and cupped like a tiny bowl.
sound: A faint whisper in the breeze.
looks: Shiny yellow, so bright it seems to glow.

name: Bluebell
touch: Little bell shaped blossoms that dangle and sway when you brush them.
sound: Almost nothing, but you would swear you hear a jingle.
looks: Drooping blue bells along a curved stem.

name: Clover blossom
touch: A fluffy round ball of tiny petals, soft as a pom pom.

looks: A pinky purple puff above three round leaves.

name: Cornflower

looks: Bright cornflower blue, the bluest flower in the meadow.

name: Poppy

looks: Bold red with a dark center.

name: Black eyed Susan

looks: Golden yellow petals around a chocolate brown eye.

sound: A soft swish as it sways.
looks: White and lacy, like a doily in the field.

name: Violet

name: Lupine
touch: A tall spike of small bumpy blossoms, like a soft corn cob.

looks: A tower of purple and pink flowers.

name: Wild rose
touch: Five soft petals, a smooth center, and careful little bumps on the stem.

name: Forget me not

name: Sunflower
