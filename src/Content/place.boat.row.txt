Smooth wooden oars rest in your paws as the tiny boat glides across the pond. Each stroke makes a soft splash, then a ripple.

The boat rocks gently as Bunny climbs in and grabs the oars. Dip, pull, glide, and the shore slips away behind you.

Cool pond water drips from the oars onto your knees. Bunny rows in slow, lazy circles around the lily pads.

The oarlocks creak with every pull, a steady wooden rhythm. The ducks paddle alongside to keep you company.

Bunny trails one paw in the water as the boat drifts. It feels cool and silky, like a ribbon.

Splash, creak, glide. Bunny rows to the middle of the pond and lets the boat turn slowly in the breeze.

The wooden seat is warm from the sun and a little bumpy. Bunny pulls the oars back and the boat surges forward.

A frog croaks from a lily pad as you row past. Bunny says hello, and the frog croaks again, very politely.

Soft ripples lap against the side of the boat. Bunny rows so gently the water barely makes a sound.

The oars feel heavy in the water, then light as they lift out. Bunny finds her rhythm and hums along.

Reeds brush the side of the boat with a whispery swish. Bunny steers around them, slow and careful.

The ducks follow in a line behind the boat like a tiny parade. Bunny rows a little slower so they can keep up.

Warm sun on your shoulders, cool water just below. Bunny rows to the shady side of the pond and rests the oars.

The boat bumps softly against a lily pad. Bunny giggles and says excuse me to it.

Dip and pull, dip and pull. The steady splash of the oars feels calming, like breathing.

A dragonfly lands on the tip of an oar and rides along. Bunny rows extra smoothly so it can stay.

The water gurgles under the boat as it glides along. Bunny says this is the smallest boat and the biggest pond.

Bunny lets go of the oars and the boat just floats, rocking softly. Rowing can wait a moment.

The pond smells green and fresh. Bunny rows past Gerald the snail, who waves from a sunny rock.

Your paws find the worn smooth grips of the oars. Bunny says this little boat has rowed a hundred happy trips.

A gentle breeze pushes the boat along all by itself. Bunny tips her head back and enjoys the free ride.

Bunny rows back to the dock with one last smooth stroke. The boat bumps softly against the wood, home again.
