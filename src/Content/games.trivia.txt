q: Who is often called the founder of psychoanalysis?

q: Learning from rewards and punishments, like a rat pressing a lever for food, is called what?

c: Imprinting

q: In Maslow's hierarchy of needs, what sits at the bottom?

d: Love and belonging

q: Who ran the Bobo doll studies, where children copied an adult hitting a doll?

c: Stanley Milgram
d: Harry Harlow
why: Bandura's Bobo doll studies showed learning by watching others, part of his social learning theory.
