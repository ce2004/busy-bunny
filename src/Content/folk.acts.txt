act: Juggling acorns
who: Pip
line: Pock, pock, pock, as acorns slap into small paws and fly up again. Pip keeps five in the air and only bonks one off her own head.

act: Tap dancing on a baking tray
who: Rocco
line: Clickety clack goes the tin tray under his toes, fast as rain on a roof. He finishes with a crow so proud the stage lights wobble.

act: The world's slowest magic trick
who: Gerald the snail
line: A long, hopeful silence, then one tiny squeak of a hat being lifted. There is a leaf underneath, exactly where he promised.

act: Grass blade whistling
who: Thistle, a visiting hare
line: A thin, bright whistle floats over the green like a happy kettle. She plays a whole tune on one blade of grass and bows to the clover.

act: Toolbox drum solo
who: Bramble
line: Clank, bonk, rattle, ting, the toolbox becomes a full drum kit. He ends by tapping a spanner on a teacup, and the crowd stamps along.

act: Poems about the moon
who: Otis
line: His low, slow voice rolls out like a warm blanket. Every poem ends with a dry little hoot that makes everyone giggle.

act: Harmonica by the sea
who: Marlow the gray seal
line: A wheezy, wobbly harmonica tune drifts out, salty and cheerful. Marlow claps his flippers for himself at the end, which is fair.

act: Synchronized quacking
who: Dot and her ducklings
line: Quack, quack quack, quack, in perfect order from big to small. The smallest duckling adds an extra quack at the end and gets the loudest cheer.

act: Reading a book upside down
who: Hazel
line: Pages rustle softly as she reads a whole fairy tale while standing on her head. Her quills tickle the stage floor and she never loses her place.

act: Lily pad cymbals
who: Fennel
line: Splat, splat, splat, two wet lily pads clap together on the beat as Fennel croaks the opening song. It is damp in the front row, and nobody minds.

act: Balancing teacups
who: Mrs. Teasel
line: Tink, tink, tink, cups clink gently as she stacks them on her shell. She walks across the stage very slowly with seven cups, and not one rattles off.

act: Bubble blowing
who: Juniper, a visiting otter
line: Soft pops fill the air as bubbles drift down and burst on whiskers and noses. She blows one so big that Pip climbs inside it for a second.

act: Knitting at top speed
who: Wooly, a visiting sheep
line: Click click click go the needles, faster than a woodpecker. By the end of the song she has made a scarf long enough for three neighbors.

act: Beatboxing
who: Chirp, a visiting cricket
line: Boom, tss, chk, boom, the tiniest performer makes the biggest beat. The whole tea house table taps along with its spoons.

act: Yodeling from the hilltop
who: Basil, a visiting mountain goat
line: A rolling yodel bounces off the hill and comes back three times. Rocco looks a little impressed and pretends he is not.

act: Impressions of everyone
who: Captain Biscuit, a visiting parrot
line: He crows like Rocco, hoots like Otis, and clanks like Bramble's toolbox. Then he does {name} yawning, and the audience howls.

act: A dramatic reading of a grocery list
who: {name}
line: A trembling, heartfelt voice reads carrots, more carrots, and one surprise turnip. {name} gets a standing ovation and takes four bows.

act: Spoon music
who: Ruffles, a visiting raccoon
line: Clackity clack, two spoons bounce off her knee in a jolly jig. She plays the spoons on the stage, a bucket and Pip's acorn, all in rhythm.

act: Musical water glasses
who: Dig, a visiting mole
line: A soft ringing hum rises as wet paws circle the rims of the glasses. Each glass sings a different note, and together they play a lullaby.

act: Interpretive dance about autumn
who: a visiting family of field mice
line: Dry leaves crunch and swirl as tiny feet twirl across the stage. The smallest mouse plays the part of an acorn and simply rolls, with great feeling.

act: Kazoo quartet
who: four visiting chipmunks
line: Bzzz, bzzz, bzzzz, four kazoos buzz in very nearly perfect harmony. It sounds like a happy beehive learning a march.

act: Apple balancing
who: Clover
line: A soft thunk as each apple lands on her nose and stays there. She balances three, then shares them with the front row, crunch crunch.

act: Tiny ukulele
who: Zip, a visiting hummingbird
line: A ukulele the size of a walnut plinks out a bright little song while wings hum like a fan. Nobody is sure how he holds it, and nobody needs to know.

act: The grand finale
who: the whole village choir
line: Every voice in the village rises together, croaks, hoots, quacks and all. The stage floor hums under your feet, and the sound wraps around you like a hug.
