title: The Squeaky Door

Clank, clank, hummm. Bramble arrives at the burrow with his toolbox. His big paw shakes your hand. It is rough and warm, like a well loved garden glove, and he smells of fresh soil.

"I hear," he rumbles, "that a door around here squeaks." {name} opens and closes its door to demonstrate. Squeeeak. Squeeeak.

Bramble listens closely, head tilted. Then he takes out a tiny oil can and gives the hinge a drop. The door swings without a sound. {name} looks thoughtful. "I liked the squeak," it admits.

Bramble chuckles, a low rumble like a big friendly cello. He loosens the hinge just a smidge. Squeak. "A small one," he says. "So you know you are home." {name} hugs his big paw.

title: The Garden Plot

Bramble takes you to his garden this week. The soil is soft and crumbly and warm from the sun. When you push your fingers in, it feels like brownie batter.

"Every friend of mine," says Bramble, "gets a plot." He shows you a little square patch, just big enough. "What would you like to grow?"

{name} does not even need to think. "Carrots," it says. "Carrots, carrots, and carrots." Bramble hums approvingly. He hands you a packet of seeds. They are tiny and rattle softly in the paper.

You plant them together, one by one, and pat the soil down gently. Bramble lets you hold the watering can. It is heavy and makes a lovely pattering sound, like a small, polite rain. "Now we wait," he says.

===
title: The Toolbox

This week Bramble lets you open his toolbox. It is heavy metal, warm from the sun, and the latch goes clunk. Inside everything clinks and rattles.

"Every tool has a name," he says. He hands you each one. A hammer with a smooth handle: "That is Harold." A wrench, cool and heavy: "Winifred." A tiny screwdriver: "Little Tom."

Then he hands you a spoon. Then another spoon. Then six more. "Why so many spoons?" asks {name}. Bramble thinks about it for a long time. "I do not know," he says. "They keep turning up. I think they like it in here."

{name} names one of the spoons Sir Stirsalot. Bramble declares it an honorary tool. It now has its own little pocket.

===
title: The First Sprouts

Bramble calls you over this week, humming louder than usual. "Come feel," he says. He guides your fingers to the soil in your garden plot.

There, poking up, are tiny soft leaves. They are feathery and delicate, like the very tip of a paintbrush. A whole little row of them.

{name} gasps so loudly that a bird nearby flaps off in surprise. "They are alive," it whispers. "My carrots are alive." It lies flat on its tummy to whisper encouragement to each one. "You are doing great. Grow big. Grow orange."

Bramble chuckles. "That helps, you know," he says. "Plants like a bit of chatter." He hums a low tune to the sprouts too. The breeze rustles the feathery leaves, and they seem to wave back.

===
title: A Bench for Resting

There is a pile of fresh planks by the garden this week. The wood smells sweet and piney, and the edges are rough until Bramble sands them smooth. Shhh, shhh, shhh goes the sandpaper.

"A bench," he says. "For resting after gardening. Every good garden needs one."

{name} helps sand. It sands one board very carefully, then gets distracted by how nice the sawdust feels and rolls in it. It comes out smelling like a pine tree. Bramble pretends not to notice and keeps humming.

By the end of the day, the bench has four steady legs and a smooth seat. It is not finished yet. "It needs a back," says Bramble, "and something special. We will see." He winks, and his toolbox clanks.

===
title: The Friendly Scarecrow

Bramble has a new project this week, and it is very squashy. You run your hands over it. A burlap sack head, stuffed with soft straw. Floppy arms. An old knitted sweater, a bit fuzzy.

"A scarecrow," says Bramble. "But I do not want it to scare anybody. I want it to welcome them."

So {name} helps make it friendly. It gives the scarecrow a big soft scarf. It gives it a straw hat with a flower tucked in. It puts a carrot in one pocket in case anybody visiting is hungry.

When the birds come by, they do not fly away. They perch on its arms and chirp. One even makes a little nest in the hat. Bramble rumbles with laughter. "Best scarecrow I ever made," he says.

===
title: The Creaky Shelf

Hazel asks Bramble to fix a library shelf this week, and you go along. The library is warm and quiet, and the shelf in question goes creak every time someone pulls out a book.

Bramble opens his toolbox very softly so the clank does not disturb any readers. He hums under his breath, so quietly you can barely hear it. Harold the hammer goes tap, tap, tap, as gently as a hammer can.

{name} holds the books while he works. It reads the titles aloud in a whisper and makes up what each one is about. Most of them, according to {name}, are about carrots.

When the shelf is done, it is smooth and steady and silent. Hazel tests it with a book. No creak at all. She gives Bramble a cup of tea as a thank you.

===
title: Gerald on Guard

Your carrots are growing tall now. The leafy tops are thick and soft, and they tickle your wrists when you reach in. Bramble says they are almost ready.

{name} is very excited. So excited it has hired a guard. Gerald the snail sits at the edge of the plot, cool and smooth and very still. "Gerald will watch them," {name} explains. "Day and night."

Gerald moves about one inch over the whole afternoon. Bramble says this is excellent patrolling, for a snail. A curious beetle wanders by, looks at Gerald, and wanders off. "See," says {name}. "He is very intimidating."

Bramble hums and waters the rows. The water patters softly, and the soil smells rich and earthy. Gerald enjoys the drops. Being a guard is thirsty work.

===
title: Harvest Day

Today is the big day. Bramble kneels beside you in the soft soil. "Hold the leafy top," he says, "and pull gently. Slow and steady."

You wrap your hand around the feathery leaves and pull. There is a soft rustle of roots, a little pop, and up comes a carrot. It is cool, a bit bumpy, and covered in crumbly soil. It is the biggest carrot you have ever felt.

{name} squeaks so loud the scarecrow's birds flutter up and settle back down. "We grew it," it says. "We grew that." It pulls one too, falls over backwards onto the soft grass, and lies there holding it up like a trophy.

Bramble chuckles and fills a whole basket. Then he says, "Now. About that bench."

===
title: The Finished Bench

The garden bench is finished. You sit on it with Bramble and {name}. The seat is smooth and warm from the sun, and the back is curved just right to lean against.

"Feel the top," says Bramble. You run your fingers along the back rail. There, carved deep enough to feel, is a little heart for you. And next to it, a carrot shape, for {name}.

"Every friend," he says, "gets a spot." {name} snuggles against your side and sighs the deepest, happiest sigh. The garden hums with bees. Water trickles from the can Bramble left tipped in the rows. The scarecrow's birds chirp.

Bramble hums along with all of it. "The happiest garden in the village," he says. "Because you helped grow it."
